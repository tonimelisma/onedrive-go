## Decisions within the brief
- One runner: `verifySummaryCollector.run` runs and times every step and prints the marker on failure; `runStepWithOutcome` (E, S, D) and `runBucket` (B) record what it returns. The buckets are the steps brought under it; order, joins and output are unchanged.
- Step names: type `verifyStepName`, a constant beside each step, hyphenated forms of today's names (`unit-tests`, `readme-status`, `e2e-full-compile`, ...); the platform build is `host-build` (another step than quick's `build`); the stress steps drop their counts (`multisync-race-repeated`, `sync-concurrency-race-repeated`, `sync-tree-race-repeated`) so a tuning change never renames a claim key. `verifyStepNames()` lists the closed set; a test holds it to the declared constants and to what every profile runs against an all-pass fake runner.
- Marker: `verify step failed: <profile> <step>` on stderr, printed by the one runner after the step returns. It starts with none of the parser's prefixes (`==> `, `--- FAIL:`, `FAIL\t`, `ok  \t`, `panic: test timed out`, `-test.shuffle `, `{`, `seed-replay: `), and its last field cannot end in `)`, so it never reads as a running test `<name> (<elapsed>)`: no line matches both.
- Reader: a failure no test names (no failed test, or a package failed without one) is keyed by each marker's `step-<job>-<step>` (job and step each through `sanitizeNightlyKey`), else by the job as today; with failed tests and no package failure, the tests alone. The package-failure case is not settled by the brief: the step keys take the job key's place there, beside the tests, which says more than the job. Only markers that start a line (after CI's timestamp), name a known profile and a step of the set count, never a `-json` event, so the public issue never carries log text (R-6.10.24). The issue lists a job's step keys as it lists test keys.
- Docs: R-6.10.23 (step keys and the marker) and R-6.10.24 (the issue may hold step keys); system.md lines 388 (stress step name), 392 (verify_summary.go), 396 (keys, marker, reader, tests cited), 653 (routing rows); CLAUDE.md:248 (keys). Sentences change, no line is reflowed.
