## C3: not built (overrun)
It is much bigger than its size-S entry. The engine writes the first blocked row of a new scope before the scope row: `persistFailureWithScopeEffects` calls `persistAndHoldFailure`, then `applyBlockScope`, and `transitionTrialScopeToPersistedBlock` does the same. With the foreign key, every scope activation would fail. Building C3 first needs the activation computed before the failure is persisted, and handed to one store method that writes the scope row and the blocked row in one transaction, on both the completion path and the trial path. On top of that come the CHECKs, upsert, release key clear, the orphan-repair and invariant deletions, the rename and the generation bump. DoR facts gathered: `foreign_keys(ON)` is already in the DSN `_pragma` of every pooled connection (driver `sqliteDriverName`); the read-only inspector (`openReadOnlySyncStoreDB`) has no pragma but is `mode=ro`/`query_only`; `UpsertBlockScope` is `INSERT OR REPLACE`; `DiscardScope` now already deletes children before the parent. The `hash_panic` rename stays with C3 because it needs the bump.
