(iv) A move: `observedItem` has no old path. Carrier chosen: `remoteItem{observedItem; OldPath string}`, the row form plus the old path, built by the converter and the orphan finders. It is not a stored field, and it is the smallest carrier: the row form is embedded, so every raw reader reads the row's own field. The projection turns each `remoteItem` into its row (when it has an item ID) and its `remoteChange{Path, OldPath, ItemType}` (always).
