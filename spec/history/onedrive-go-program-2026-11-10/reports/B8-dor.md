# B8 Definition of Ready: watch runners carry an identity, every stop is bounded, a stuck drive fails closed

Worktree `~/src/onedrive-go-wt/watch-runner-stop`, branch `fix/watch-runner-stop`, rebased onto `34fb1a66` (written at `05336a1a`; items 3, 5, 6 and 7 corrected by the second writer against the code; items 1, 2, 5 and 6 widened for the second pass, which fixed the first pass's seven "Not fixed" entries at the reviewer's request).

1. PASS **Task intent**: a watch never waits without bound for a drive to stop, and never applies one runner's event to another. Runners and their events carry a generation (stale events are ignored); one `retireWatchRunner` does every cancel/wait/close/delete with a bound on the stop and on the engine close; a runner that misses either bound is marked stuck, left in `runners` (nothing is started under its ID, its engine is not closed under it), logged once at Error with every goroutine's stack, and `RunWatch` returns `ErrSyncStuck` (renamed from `ErrWatchRunnerStuck`; exit 1). Second pass: one-shot engine closes use the same bound; every detached store write in `internal/sync` carries a deadline; `drive rebind-root` joins the reset under the folder locks (folder at the path and the recorded folder); `WatchReloaded` states its outcome. Runner event sends also give up on the runner's own context; the child run mode is part of runner equivalence; the control-socket owner lock is released only after the runners have drained; `drive reset-sync-state` takes the drive's sync-root lock.
