# W19 Definition of Ready: `status` answers within a bound when OneDrive is slow

1. PASS **Task intent**: `onedrive-go status` prints within one budget (10 s, all accounts together) whatever OneDrive does with its live requests (`GET /me`, `/me/drive`, `/drives/{id}`): no answer, a slow answer, or a server error. The accounts are asked at the same time. An account whose answers did not arrive in the budget, or whose `/me` failed for a reason other than sign-in, shows its local state, and stderr carries one plain line per such account with the reason; `--json` stdout stays one document; exit 0. A sign-in failure (401) keeps its present handling.
